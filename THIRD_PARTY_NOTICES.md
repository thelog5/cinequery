## IMDb

The database is built from [IMDb non-commercial datasets](https://developer.imdb.com/non-commercial-datasets/). Movie data is courtesy of IMDb and is used for personal, non-commercial purposes under those dataset terms. Full database dumps and downloaded datasets are not distributed in this repository. Saved evaluations contain movie questions and limited query results for reproducibility.

Review IMDb's terms before commercializing the deployed service or redistributing data. A permissive application-code license does not grant commercial IMDb data rights.
